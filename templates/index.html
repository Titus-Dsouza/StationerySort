Open-Weight Gemma AI Architecture

🌟 Why Local Open-Weight Inference?

StationerySort integrates Google's open-weight Gemma models locally via Ollama. Rather than sending student inventory data across the internet to paid third-party APIs, local execution provides:

  • Model Choice & Freedom: Swap seamlessly between gemma4:12b, gemma2:2b, or other open-weight models based on device capability.
  • Zero Cost & No Rate Limits: Completely free to run with no API keys, credit cards, or subscription tiers.
  • Privacy First: Student supplies, personal notes, and room/desk locations stay strictly on the user's computer.

🛡️ Human-in-the-Loop Confirmation

Local AI models are powerful assistants, but in inventory systems, accidental edits cause stock discrepancies. StationerySort enforces a strict Proposal & Confirm workflow: Gemma interprets natural language into structured actions, and changes are only committed to SQLite once the student reviews and confirms them.

🔄 Graceful Fallback Engine

If Ollama is not currently running or the model is still loading, StationerySort's built-in heuristic rule engine immediately takes over. The student can still manage their supplies without interruption, and the UI transparently signals whether the proposal came from Gemma or the fallback engine.
