## 🎬 Step-by-Step Demo Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Initial Dashboard & Sample Data Verification
1. Observe the top navigation bar:
   - Notice the green status badge: **`● Gemma (gemma4:12b) Online`** (or `Offline Fallback Engine` if Ollama is not running).
   - Click the **"Why Local Gemma?"** button to view the modal explaining open-weight local inference, zero API fees, privacy benefits, and human-in-the-loop confirmation.
2. Review the **4 Stat Cards**:
   - Unique Items: `12`
   - Total Units: `58`
   - Needs Restocking: `6` (highlighted in warm terracotta)
   - Categories: `5`
3. Scroll through the cards:
   - Notice that items like **"Blue Gel Pens"** (Qty: 2, Min: 5) and **"Pastel Sticky Notes"** (Qty: 1, Min: 3) have an alert badge: **`⚠️ Low Stock`** and an alert progress bar.

---

### Scenario 2: Testing the Open-Weight Gemma AI Assistant
1. In the **Natural Language AI Assistant** panel, look at the quick prompt chips.
2. Click the quick chip: **`✏️ Bought 10 blue pens`** (or type `"I bought 10 new blue pens"`).
3. Click the **"Interpret"** button.
   - The button switches to a spinner: *"Interpreting..."*.
   - In a few seconds, the **Review Proposed Inventory Change** card animates into view.
4. Verify the proposal:
   - **Source**: `Local Gemma (gemma4:12b)`
   - **Proposed Action**: `ADD STOCK`
   - **Target Item**: `Blue Gel Pens (0.5mm)`
   - **Category**: `Writing`
   - **Stock Transition**: `2 pcs ➔ 12 pcs (+10)`
   - **Explanation**: A friendly student explanation generated by Gemma.
5. Notice the bottom disclaimer: *"⚠️ No changes are applied until you confirm."*
6. Click **"Confirm & Apply Update"**:
   - A success toast notification appears: *"Successfully updated 'Blue Gel Pens (0.5mm)' to 12 pcs."*
   - Scroll down to the Blue Gel Pens card: notice that the quantity has updated to **12 pcs**, and the `⚠️ Low Stock` alert badge is automatically cleared because 12 > min stock (5)!

---

### Scenario 3: Testing Usage Deduction & Low-Stock Trigger
1. In the AI Assistant prompt box, click the chip: **`📝 Used 2 sticky note pads`** (or type `"Used 2 sticky note pads"`).
2. Click **"Interpret"**.
3. Verify the proposal:
   - **Action**: `USE STOCK`
   - **Target Item**: `Pastel Sticky Notes (3x3 in)`
   - **Stock Transition**: `1 pads ➔ 0 pads (-2)`
   - **Notice Box**: *"Notice: This change will put or keep this stationery item below its minimum threshold!"*
4. Click **"Confirm & Apply Update"**:
   - The sticky notes count drops to `0 pads`, clearly indicating that the student has run out of sticky notes and needs restocking.

---

### Scenario 4: Adding a Completely New Item via AI
1. In the AI Assistant prompt box, type:
   ```
   Found 3 metallic calligraphy pens in locker
   ```
2. Click **"Interpret"**.
3. Verify that Gemma recognizes this as a new item:
   - **Action**: `CREATE NEW ITEM`
   - **Target Item**: `Metallic Calligraphy Pens`
   - **Category**: Automatically categorized under `Writing`
   - **Quantity**: `3 pcs`
4. Click **"Confirm & Apply Update"**:
   - The new stationery item is added to SQLite and appears immediately in the Writing category.

---

### Scenario 5: Interactive Search, Category Filters, and Quantity Steppers
1. **Live Search**:
   - In the search bar, type `pencil`. Notice instant filtering to show only HB pencils. Clear the search.
2. **Category Filter**:
   - Click the **`Writing`** pill to see only writing instruments. Click **`All Items`** to reset.
3. **Low-Stock Quick Filter**:
   - Click the terracotta **"Needs Restocking"** stat card.
   - The list immediately filters to only show items requiring restocking.
4. **Card Steppers**:
   - On any card, click the **`+`** button to increment quantity by 1.
   - Click the **`-`** button to decrement quantity by 1.
   - Notice the instant feedback and updated progress track.

---

### Scenario 6: Resetting Back to Clean Sample Data
1. Click the **"Reset Sample"** button in the top navigation header.
2. Confirm the prompt dialog.
3. The inventory resets back to the original 12 standard student stationery items.
